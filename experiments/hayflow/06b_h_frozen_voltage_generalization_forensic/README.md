# HayFlow 06b-h - frozen voltage-generalization forensic

The 06b-g STATE curriculum learned a useful recursive intervention, but none
of its candidates beat voltage persistence on the independent train component.
This experiment does not train another neural model. It freezes four registered
checkpoint families and asks whether their voltage failure is a correctable
amplitude/bias calibration problem or a representation-generalization problem.

The seventh unused connected component of every train regime is calibration
only. The eighth is a sealed audit role and is not used for candidate selection.
The matrix crosses four frozen model arms, five voltage shrinkage factors and
two global-bias modes. It also reports errors separately for quiescent,
moderate, active and regenerative voltage transitions.

A positive audit can authorize only a later train-only voltage-objective
recalibration. A failure sends the project back to an atomic voltage
representation playground. This experiment never authorizes 06c, validation
or test access, fresh-test generation, full training or mass-data generation.

Notebook: `notebooks/06b_h_frozen_voltage_generalization_forensic.ipynb`.
